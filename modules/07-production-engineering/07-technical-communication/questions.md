# Knowledge check — Technical Communication

- Explain Design docs in your own words and give a concrete example.
- Explain Performance reports in your own words and give a concrete example.
- Explain Trade-offs in your own words and give a concrete example.
- What would you predict before running this lab: Write a design memo and a measured project report?
- What evidence would support or contradict your explanation?
- Demonstrate the exit criterion: Defend decisions, limitations and next steps.
