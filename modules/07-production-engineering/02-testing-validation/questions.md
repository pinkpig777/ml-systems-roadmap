# Knowledge check — Testing & Validation

- Explain Unit in your own words and give a concrete example.
- Explain Integration in your own words and give a concrete example.
- Explain Numerical correctness in your own words and give a concrete example.
- What would you predict before running this lab: Test success, invalid inputs and numerical tolerances?
- What evidence would support or contradict your explanation?
- Demonstrate the exit criterion: Run tests reproducibly and explain coverage limits.
